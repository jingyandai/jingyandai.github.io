We examine whether policy-mandated IT adoption creates genuine organizational value beyond regulatory compliance. Using a panel of 1,384 U.S. hospitals from 2012 to 2016 and staggered difference-in-differences designs, we find that the meaningful use of electronic health records improves emergency department efficiency by reducing wait times for discharged patients, though not for inpatients, and significantly enhances patient satisfaction. The gains diminish in hospitals handling more complex caseloads, revealing important boundary conditions for IT-enabled value realization and informing the design of future health IT policies.
