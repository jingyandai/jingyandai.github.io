In Fall 2025, I was the instructor of record for QMB 4702, Managerial Operations Analysis 2, a required senior-level core course with 52 students. I was responsible for the course end to end: learning objectives, lecture slides, in-class Excel demonstrations, homework, quizzes, exams, and the Canvas site.

The course is the second half of the college's management science sequence, covering decision analysis under uncertainty (payoff and regret tables, decision criteria, decision trees, and the value of information), utility theory and risk attitudes, queuing models, and project planning and control using CPM and PERT. I built every model live in Excel with my students and introduced each technique through a managerial decision it can inform. Students described me in official evaluations as a "kind and patient teacher" whose classes are "very well done, in a linear way, making it easy to follow the path."
